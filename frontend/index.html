SECTION 03

Multi-Temporal Change Detection Operational

Tri-epoch multispectral change characterization comparing identical spatial footprints across 2024 (Sentinel-2B), 2025 (Sentinel-2B), and 2026 (Sentinel-2C).

Spectral Index Formulations:
ΔNDVI = [(B08-B04)/(B08+B04)]later − [(B08-B04)/(B08+B04)]earlier
ΔNDWI = [(B03-B08)/(B03+B08)]later − [(B03-B08)/(B03+B08)]earlier
ΔBrightness = [μ(RGB)later − μ(RGB)earlier] / μ(RGB)earlier
TypologyRule-Based Spectral GatingPhysical Interpretation

Vegetation GainΔNDVI > +0.20Agricultural crop development, seasonal greening, or re-vegetation.

SECTION 04

8-Stage Preprocessing & Radiometric Lab Operational

Deterministic 8-stage image conditioning pipeline converting raw Level-2A Copernicus granules into radiometrically comparable, sub-pixel registered tiles.

StageDesignationTechnical Process
01Metadata ExtractionParses MTD_MSIL2A.xml; extracts solar zenith/azimuth, orbit, and granule metadata.
02CRS VerificationValidates UTM Zone 45N (EPSG:32645), computes affine geotransform and WGS-84 bounds.
03SCL Cloud MaskingFilters Scene Classification Layer: class 3 (shadow), 8 (med cloud), 9 (high cloud), 10 (cirrus).
04Quality AssessmentEnforces ≥ 95% valid pixel ratio threshold; computes signal-to-noise ratio proxy (8.22).
05Radiometric CalScales BOA reflectance from integer [0, 10000] to physical float32 range [0.0, 1.0].
06Sub-Pixel Co-RegFourier 2D phase correlation cross-power spectrum; checks shift (Δx, Δy ≤ 0.5 px).
07Phenology / IllumSubtracts regional background drift (μpheno = -0.13) and normalizes solar zenith angle.
08Spatial TilingUniform 256×256 pixel chipping at 10m native spatial resolution with georeferencing.
